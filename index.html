Project Overview

Journi is a destination-based travel memory system developed in collaboration with Turkish Airlines. The project transforms travel souvenirs into meaningful keepsakes by combining a physical memory kit with a digital companion application. Rather than storing memories in drawers, Journi encourages travellers to preserve, organize and revisit every journey through one unified experience.

Why Turkish Airlines?

Turkish Airlines was selected as the project partner because of its extensive destination network and its ability to connect cultures across the world. This collaboration demonstrates how a single product system can adapt to different locations while maintaining one consistent travel experience.

05 / UNITS

THE UNITS

01 / MOTHER SHIP

NAVORA

The mobile command base, mothership and data hub of the KOR research ecosystem.

01 COMMAND BASE
02 MOTHERSHIP
03 DATA HUB

NAVORA forms the central operational platform of the KOR ecosystem. It acts as a mobile command base and mothership while functioning as the main hub for research and data exchange.

During a research mission, researchers arrive at the harbour, take on supplies and travel towards the volcanic island. Once on site, the vessel supports the research operation, communication and laboratory activities while IGNIS is deployed for field operations.

02 / RESEARCH ROVER

IGNIS

An unmanned ground vehicle designed to operate across hazardous volcanic terrain.

01 UNMANNED
02 FIELD RESEARCH
03 ATMOSPHERIC ANALYSIS
04 360° SCANNING

IGNIS is deployed from NAVORA to carry out research in areas that are difficult or dangerous for researchers to access directly. Its role is to navigate treacherous terrain and perform detailed in-situ atmospheric analysis.

During the mission, IGNIS goes ashore, collects samples and performs 360° field scanning. Visual data is sent back to the research system, while collected samples are transferred for further analysis in the laboratory.

THE PROJECT

A design research project exploring how people and stray dogs encounter each other in shared urban spaces.

FOCUS

Understanding fear, distance, behaviour and communication between humans and dogs.

01
RESEARCH DIRECTION

In order to make some decisions during our design research, we had certain headings that we needed to determine first, and we revised them over time to reach our final decision.

PROCESS 01 01
Broad Topic:

Human-Dog Encounters on City Streets

Focused Topic:

Developing design to foster mutual understanding and trust between people and stray dogs in the rush of İzmr city streets

Design Research Problem:

How can a design solution be developed that helps people rushing to work better understand stray dogs and their behavior,in order to build mutual trust in public spaces?

Keywords:
Public spaces human-dog trust building empathy in urban design urban human-dogs interaction urban rush in İzmir fear management behavioral insights stray dogs behavior in İzmir

Keywords:

How can the design of bus stops in Izmir be used to increase public awareness about maintaining safe distances from stray dogs and encourage mutual respect between humans and dogs in crowded transit areas?

Keywords:

There is a fairly crowded and dynamic movement of people in this area. People in this area often seem bored or flustered from waiting.

13 / USABILITY REVIEW
FROM OBSERVATION TO ITERATION

WHAT COULD MAKE THE EXPERIENCE CLEARER?

Reviewing the prototype helped identify where the experience felt intuitive and where the connection between story and food could become stronger.

01
DISCOVERY

USERS UNDERSTOOD THE IDEA QUICKLY.

Story, movie and character references created an immediate entry point into food discovery.

WHAT CHANGED

Story-led discovery remains the primary entry point.

02
CONNECTION

THE STORY-TO-RECIPE TRANSITION BECAME THE CRITICAL MOMENT.

The experience needed to make the relationship between the selected scene and dish unmistakable.

WHAT CHANGED

The visual connection between scene, food and recipe was strengthened.

03
PERSONALIZATION

PERSONALIZATION WORKED BETTER AFTER A DISH WAS SELECTED.

Preferences become more meaningful when users already have something they want to make.

WHAT CHANGED

Personalization was positioned after recipe selection.

KEY TAKEAWAY

THE EXPERIENCE BECAME CLEARER BY CONNECTING STORY, DISCOVERY AND ACTION.
